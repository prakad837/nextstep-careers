About NextStep Careers

Created from real international-student experience in Japan

NextStep Careers was created to support international students who are preparing to begin their professional careers in Japan.

We understand that Japanese resumes, self-PR, motivation statements, professional emails, applications, and interviews can feel confusing for students using a second or third language.

Our goal is to provide clear, practical, and personalized support based on each student's real education, experience, Japanese level, target industry, and career goals.

  • Support is based on real information provided by the customer.
  • Documents are personalized for the customer's actual background.
  • No job, interview, visa, or application result is guaranteed.
  • Personal information is used only to provide the requested service.
No fake experience. No fake documents. No guaranteed job promises. Only honest and practical career support.
